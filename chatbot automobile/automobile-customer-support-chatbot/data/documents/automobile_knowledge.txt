AUTOMOBILE CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

This document provides general automobile customer support information.
It covers vehicle maintenance, engine oil, tyres, battery, brakes,
overheating, troubleshooting, servicing and warranty.

CHAPTER 2: REGULAR VEHICLE MAINTENANCE

Regular automobile maintenance helps improve vehicle reliability,
performance and safety.

Important maintenance activities include:
1. Checking engine oil.
2. Checking coolant level.
3. Inspecting tyre pressure.
4. Checking brake condition.
5. Inspecting battery condition.
6. Checking lights and indicators.
7. Inspecting air filters.
8. Following the manufacturer's service schedule.

The vehicle owner's manual should always be followed for
model-specific maintenance intervals.

CHAPTER 3: ENGINE OIL

Engine oil lubricates moving engine components and helps reduce friction.

General engine oil maintenance guidelines:
1. Check the oil level according to the owner's manual.
2. Park the vehicle on a level surface before checking.
3. Use the oil grade recommended by the manufacturer.
4. Replace engine oil according to the service schedule.
5. Investigate unusual oil leakage immediately.

The correct oil grade and replacement interval depend on
the vehicle model and engine type.

CHAPTER 4: TYRE MAINTENANCE

Tyres provide road grip, stability and braking support.

Important tyre maintenance practices:
1. Check tyre pressure regularly.
2. Follow the recommended pressure mentioned in the vehicle manual.
3. Inspect tyres for cuts, cracks and unusual wear.
4. Check tyre tread condition.
5. Follow manufacturer recommendations for tyre rotation.
6. Use the correct tyre size for the vehicle.

Incorrect tyre pressure can affect handling, fuel efficiency
and tyre life.

CHAPTER 5: BATTERY PROBLEMS

A vehicle battery supplies electrical power for starting
and operating electrical systems.

Common signs of battery problems:
1. Engine does not start.
2. Slow engine cranking.
3. Dim headlights.
4. Battery warning light.
5. Electrical accessories behave irregularly.

Possible causes include an aged battery, loose connections
or a charging system problem.

A qualified technician should inspect the battery and
charging system.

CHAPTER 6: BRAKING SYSTEM

The braking system is essential for vehicle safety.

Possible signs of brake problems:
1. Unusual noise while braking.
2. Brake pedal feels unusually soft.
3. Vehicle pulls to one side during braking.
4. Brake warning light appears.
5. Reduced braking performance.

If the brakes are not functioning properly, stop the vehicle
at a safe location and contact qualified roadside assistance.
Do not continue driving with suspected brake failure.

CHAPTER 7: ENGINE OVERHEATING

Engine overheating may occur due to cooling system problems,
low coolant, radiator issues or other mechanical faults.

Warning signs may include:
1. Temperature warning light.
2. Temperature gauge entering the red zone.
3. Steam from the engine compartment.
4. Unusual engine performance.

If overheating occurs:
1. Stop at a safe location.
2. Switch off the engine when safe.
3. Allow the engine to cool.
4. Never open a hot radiator cap.
5. Contact roadside assistance or a qualified technician.

CHAPTER 8: VEHICLE SERVICING

Vehicle servicing helps maintain performance and reliability.

A general service inspection may include:
1. Engine oil and filter inspection.
2. Brake inspection.
3. Tyre inspection.
4. Battery inspection.
5. Coolant inspection.
6. Air filter inspection.
7. Lights and electrical system inspection.
8. General vehicle safety inspection.

Service intervals vary by manufacturer, vehicle model,
driving conditions and usage.

CHAPTER 9: WARRANTY INFORMATION

Vehicle warranty conditions vary according to the manufacturer,
vehicle model, purchase date and warranty policy.

Customers should check:
1. Warranty start date.
2. Warranty duration.
3. Covered components.
4. Excluded components.
5. Maintenance requirements.
6. Authorized service centre conditions.

Customers should consult their official warranty booklet
or authorized dealer for accurate coverage information.

CHAPTER 10: COMMON CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

Q1. How often should engine oil be checked?

Answer: Engine oil should be checked regularly according
to the manufacturer's recommendations.

Q2. Why is my car not starting?

Answer: Possible causes include a weak battery, starter
system issue or fuel system problem. A technician should
inspect the vehicle.

Q3. What should I do if my engine overheats?

Answer: Stop safely, switch off the engine when safe,
allow it to cool and contact roadside assistance.

Q4. Why is tyre pressure important?

Answer: Correct tyre pressure supports handling, braking,
fuel efficiency and tyre life.

Q5. What should I do if my brakes make unusual noise?

Answer: Arrange a professional brake inspection.
If braking performance is reduced, stop safely and
contact roadside assistance.

Q6. How can I check warranty coverage?

Answer: Refer to the official warranty booklet or contact
the vehicle manufacturer or authorized dealer.

CHAPTER 11: CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDELINES

The automobile customer support assistant should:
1. Understand the customer's query.
2. Identify the customer's intent.
3. Retrieve relevant information from the knowledge base.
4. Provide clear and helpful answers.
5. Avoid inventing technical specifications.
6. Recommend qualified technicians for complex problems.
7. Prioritize customer and vehicle safety.
